Royal Style Hair Studio was started by a barber who believes a good haircut is equal parts skill and attention — not a rushed ten-minute job. With seven-plus years behind the chair, every cut is worked out for the client's face, hair type and how much time they actually have to style it each morning.

The shop keeps things simple: clean tools, honest prices, and no waiting around for someone who doesn't show up. It's become a regular stop for students from nearby colleges, working professionals, and residents who've been coming back for years.
